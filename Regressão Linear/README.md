Linear regression is a fundamental tool in data analysis, used to understand the relationship between variables. Simply put, it helps to predict an outcome (dependent variable) from one or more input variables (independent variables). It is a powerful and widely used technique for forecasting trends and making estimations.

During my studies, I applied a linear regression model to analyze a dataset on residential energy consumption. This dataset, collected from a house in Sceaux, France, and made available by the UCI Machine Learning repository, allowed me to understand how factors like active electrical power and voltage were related to the total energy consumption.

By using linear regression, I was able to identify patterns and even predict consumption in different parts of the house, such as the kitchen or laundry room, based on general measurements. This demonstrates the practical importance of linear regression for predicting and optimizing the use of resources, like electricity, in real-world contexts.
